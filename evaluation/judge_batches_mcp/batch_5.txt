## Cause

The fixed worker logic computes **`completedAttemptCount = job.attemptCount + 1` first**, then uses that for **`nextAttemptDate(completedAttemptCount)`**, persistence, and max-attempt / dead handling—so the **next delay matches the attempt that actually completed**, not the previous stored count.
